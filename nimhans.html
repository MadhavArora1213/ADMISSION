About NIMHANS

🏛️ Overview

The National Institute of Mental Health and Neuro Sciences (NIMHANS) is a premier medical institution and a hospital in Bengaluru, India. It is an Institute of National Importance under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India. It is the nodal centre for mental health education, research, training, and advanced tertiary care in the country.

✨ Why Choose NIMHANS?

  • Only specialised neuroscience institute with National Importance status in India
  • 800+ bedded super speciality hospital with 30+ departments
  • World-class research infrastructure and international collaborations
  • Highly subsidised government fee structure — accessible to all
  • NAAC A+ accredited; regularly ranked among India's top institutions
  • Stipends and fellowships available for research scholars
  • Hostel facility available for students on campus
